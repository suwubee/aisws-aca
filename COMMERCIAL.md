# Commercial Use & Enterprise

AISWS-ACA（AI超站）is open source under the Apache License 2.0 (`LICENSE`).

## What requires authorization

Apache-2.0 allows commercial use of the code. However, **using AISWS trademarks/branding** for commercial distribution or hosting requires permission (see `TRADEMARKS.md`).

## Suggested enterprise roadmap (non-binding)

This repo focuses on the community edition. Typical enterprise/commercial directions include:

- SSO (OIDC/SAML), SCIM user provisioning, MFA and advanced RBAC
- Multi-tenant isolation (org/workspace), audit retention & compliance exports
- Secret management integrations (Vault/KMS), encryption-at-rest, key rotation
- Observability integrations (metrics/traces/logs), alerting & incident workflow
- Runbook governance (approval policies, policy-as-code, change windows)
- Visual OS operations: Windows (RDP/WinRM/PowerShell), Linux desktop (VNC), and safe GUI automation

For commercial authorization, enterprise support, or OEM distribution, contact the maintainers.
